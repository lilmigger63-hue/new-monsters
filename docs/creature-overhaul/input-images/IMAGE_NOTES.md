# Illustration notes

The 4 artwork panels were created for this conversation to provide original, dark-fantasy moodboard imagery. Their role is *visual inspiration*, not licensed reproductions of the Internet images shown by dynamic image searches, and not assets ready for distribution in the Minecraft mod.

| File | Subject | What to study |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `01_ancient_treant.png` | Ancient Treant | Twisted roots, moss, branch crown, amber eyes, organic volume |
| `02_grave_knight.png` | Grave Knight | Heavy armor silhouette, tattered cape, blue spectral accents, sword weight |
| `03_moon_stag.png` | Moon Stag | Natural deer proportions, elaborate branching antlers, luminous cool accents |
| `04_demon_sorceress.png` | Demon Sorceress | Horns, dark robe silhouette, violet concentric magical circles and casting gestures |

`00_full_moodboard.jpg` combines the four panels.

The complete implementation prompt is in `../CODEX_PROMPT.md` and requires research for every species. These four example panels should not be mistaken for a complete research atlas or for source-attributed third-party concept art.
